Certificate Renewal → Sales Opportunity Flow

From expiration detection in DCS to a consolidated Salesforce opportunity for the account owner

System
📄
DCS Detects a certificate approaching expiration and generates a renewal notice.
  • Certificate + product data
  • Expiration date identified
➜
Integration
🔄
OIC Receives the alert, combines related certificates, and maps ownership.
  • Consolidates multiple certs per account
  • Maps Account Owner & COU
  • Populates custom certificate field
Business Logic Applied
➜
CRM
💼
Salesforce Creates one consolidated opportunity with certificate details attached.
  • One opportunity per account (not per cert)
  • Custom field carries cert details
➜
Person
🧑‍💼
Account Owner Reviews the opportunity and reaches out proactively before expiration.
  • Sees all expiring certs at once
  • Initiates renewal conversation
➜
Customer
🧑
Customer Receives a timely, consolidated renewal conversation instead of multiple disconnected notices.

Customer
Multiple expiring certificates for the same account are consolidated into a single Salesforce opportunity by OIC's business logic, reducing duplicate outreach and giving the account owner one clear, complete view of the renewal need.
