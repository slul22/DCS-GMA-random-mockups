CRC Process Review

Current State: Observations & Process

Where the CRC process stands today across the NA scheme, IEC assessments, and PrE engagement — and the key gaps driving our next steps.

Current CRC Process

  • Tied to file/volume numbers, not certifications.
  • One file/volume may cover multiple certifications, impacting quotes.
  • Many items are TBD; customers must confirm product impact.
  • Some models may be EOL.

IEC

  • PrE review is inconsistent.
  • Execution varies: some assessments are completed, others are not.

Where We Ended

  • Engage PrEs on IEC standard assessments and align with the existing NA scheme process.
  • Revise charter to emphasize business growth/monetization; next step is alignment with Jason and Christian.

Key Observations

1PrEs view the RMS CRC process as labor intensive and overly complex.
2PrE buy-in is needed to support GCRCs.
3Several active projects have overlapping scopes.
4Report data must reach clause-by-clause detail.
5The NA process is tied to volume/section rather than certifications, which affects quote/project routing.
6Straight-to-order needs VBP implementation — still in pilot phase and not yet live in all COUs.
