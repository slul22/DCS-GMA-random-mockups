Certificate Renewal → Sales Opportunity Flow

DCS

Certificate expiration
is detected.

DCS identifies certificates approaching expiration and generates a renewal notice with product data.

OIC

Alerts are combined
and enriched.

OIC consolidates related certificates per account and maps Account Owner and COU into a custom field.

Salesforce

One opportunity
is created.

A single, consolidated opportunity is created per account, carrying certificate details in a custom field.

Account Owner

Rep reviews and
reaches out.

The account owner sees all expiring certificates at once and starts the renewal conversation.

Customer

Customer gets one
clear renewal ask.

One timely, consolidated conversation instead of multiple disconnected notices.
