DCS  →  OIC  →  Salesforce

Certificate Renewal → Sales Opportunity Flow

How a certificate expiration in DCS becomes a single, consolidated renewal opportunity — routed to the right account owner in Salesforce.

Step 1 · System
DCS

Detects a certificate approaching expiration and generates a renewal notice.

  • Certificate & product data
  • Expiration date identified
Step 2 · Integration
OIC

Applies business logic to consolidate and enrich the alert.

  • Combines multiple certs per account
  • Maps Account Owner & COU
  • Populates custom cert field
BUSINESS LOGIC
Step 3 · CRM
Salesforce

Creates one consolidated opportunity with certificate details attached.

  • One opportunity per account
  • Custom field carries cert detail
Step 4 · Person
Account Owner

Reviews the opportunity and reaches out proactively before expiration.

  • Sees all expiring certs at once
  • Initiates renewal conversation
Step 5 · Customer
Customer

Receives one timely, consolidated renewal conversation instead of scattered notices.

Fewer duplicate opportunities

Consolidation logic merges related certificate expirations into a single opportunity per account.

Faster renewal outreach

Account owners are notified automatically, ahead of expiration, with full certificate context.

Accurate ownership routing

OIC maps Account Owner and COU so the right rep engages the right customer, every time.
