Versioning + Relationship & Lineage Framework
for News Records

Establish two foundational capabilities in GMA News: in-place editorial versioning of a published article for typos, clarifications, and non-breaking corrections — and a relationship & lineage framework that defines how distinct articles connect to one another. These are deliberately separate concepts: versioning preserves history within a single article record, while relationships link separate articles that each represent their own regulatory event.

Problem Statement

Two distinct gaps
Editorial Gap

No version history on published articles

  • Approved articles are silently editable with no change record
  • No history of what changed, when, or by whom
  • RI customers and DCS cannot detect if a reviewed article was altered
  • Internal teams have no audit trail to defend editorial decisions
Relationship Gap

No system-level connections between articles

  • No link when a draft becomes finalized as a separate article
  • No link when a correction or consolidated update is issued
  • Customers see duplicative or stale content with no clear "current" indicator
  • No lineage chain for RI or DCS to act on
  • No versioning — edits to approved articles are silent and untracked
  • No audit trail — no record of who changed what or when
  • Editable after approval — published articles have no protection against silent overwrites

These are listed as known limitations in the GMA News README today. This epic resolves all three.

Core Capabilities

3 capability groups
A In-Place Editorial Versioning · Single article
  • Every approved article carries a version number; edits create a new version (e.g., v1 → v2) rather than silently overwriting
  • Editors classify each new version as one of two types:
    • Minor revision — typo, formatting, or link fix (non-substantive)
    • Substantive revision — content, metadata, dates, or impact changes that customers should know about
  • Required editor-authored "Revision note" describing what changed and why
  • Full version history retained and viewable internally with prior-version snapshots and field-level diffs
  • Only the latest published version is exposed to downstream consumers; superseded versions are not distributed
  • Substantive revisions send an event to RI and DCS — minor revisions update in place without a downstream notification (still recorded in history)
B Relationship & Lineage Framework · Across articles
  • Define directional and peer relationships between approved articles:
    • supersedes / superseded-by
    • correction-to / corrected-by
    • consolidates / consolidated-into
    • related coverage
    • references / cited-by
  • Capture a "What changed" summary on the superseding article when a supersedes relationship is established
  • Automatically mark predecessor articles inactive when a superseding or consolidating article is approved
  • Author relationships within the existing news creation workflow — with article search, validation, and lineage preview
  • Maintain an internal audit trail of relationship changes and active/inactive state transitions
  • Publish relationship and lineage data to RI and DCS through existing distribution channels
C Shared Foundation · Cross-cutting

Unified Audit Log

  • Single audit log spanning version history, relationship edits, and state transitions
  • Every change timestamped, attributed to a user, and permanently retained

Role-Based Permissions

  • Originator, Reviewer, Admin override, Reader applied consistently
  • Reviewer approval required before a new version goes live

Relationship Types

5 types
Type Inverse Meaning Predecessor Behavior
Supersedes Superseded-by New article fully replaces predecessor as the authoritative record Predecessor automatically set to Inactive on approval
Correction-to Corrected-by New article corrects a factual or substantive error in predecessor Predecessor remains Active; correction surfaced alongside
Consolidates Consolidated-into New article merges content from multiple predecessor articles All predecessors automatically set to Inactive on approval
Related Coverage Related Coverage Peer articles covering the same regulatory event from different angles No state change — both articles remain Active
References Cited-by Article cites another as supporting context or source material No state change — both articles remain Active
🛡️

Validation rules enforced at authoring time: cycle prevention (Article A cannot supersede Article B if B already supersedes A), duplicate pair prevention, and contradictory pairing prevention — zero invalid relationships reach an approved state.

Draft Feature Outline

9 features
1

Article Version Model

Version number, minor/substantive classification, revision note, snapshot retention, and field-level diff storage per version.

2

Edit-After-Approval Workflow

Creates a new version on edit of a published article, requires a revision note, routes through Reviewer approval before the new version goes live.

3

Relationship Data Model & Validation

Persisted relationship pairs with type, direction, audit metadata, and validation rules (cycle prevention, inverse pairing, duplicate prevention).

4

Active/Inactive Flag & "What Changed" Field

Boolean active/inactive state on every article record, plus a "What changed" free-text summary authored by the editor on the superseding article.

5

Relationships Step in Authoring Workflow

New step in the news creation/edit flow — article search by title/ID, relationship type selection, and real-time lineage preview before submission.

6

Approval-Time Behavior

On approval of a superseding or consolidating article, automatically flip predecessor active → inactive and emit the appropriate lineage event.

7

Unified Audit Log

Single structured log spanning version history, relationship edits, and state changes — every entry time-stamped and user-attributed.

8

Role-Based Permissions

Originator, Reviewer, Admin override, and Reader roles applied consistently to all versioning and relationship-authoring actions.

9

Outbound Contract Updates (RI & DCS)

New payload fields and events for downstream consumers — new fields: version, revision_type, revision_note, relationships, is_active, change_summary, lineage_chain_ids; new events: article.revised, article.superseded, article.corrected, article.consolidated.

Out of Scope

MVP exclusions
"Follow-up to" relationship type
System-suggested or auto-detected relationships
Bulk historical backfill of relationships or version history across the existing article corpus
Customer-facing audit log of internal editorial revisions or relationship edits (downstream apps surface only the user-visible change indicators)
Branching or parallel versions of an article (linear history only)
Editorial revision merging across multiple in-flight edits (single-editor-at-a-time pessimistic locking acceptable for MVP)

Success Criteria

7 criteria
✓
Editors can author all five relationship types end-to-end without engineering support
✓
Editors can revise a published article through the new versioning workflow without engineering support
✓
100% of edits to approved articles produce a new version with revision type and revision note captured
✓
100% of newly approved superseding articles correctly flip predecessor state and publish a lineage event
✓
Zero invalid relationships (cycles, duplicates, contradictory pairs) reach an approved state
✓
RI and DCS successfully consume version events and lineage events for every approved change in integration testing
✓
Editor-reported time to publish a successor article or revise an existing article is acceptable per usability review

Risks & Considerations

6 items
Authoring Burden

Adds steps to the editor workflow for both versioning and relationships

Mitigate: required-field prompts only when needed; contextual guidance in-flow

Revision Type Judgment

Minor vs. substantive classification is editor-driven and may be inconsistent

Mitigate: examples in tooling, reviewer oversight on substantive revisions

Data Quality Dependency

Downstream value depends entirely on editors choosing correct types and classifications

Mitigate: validation rules, reviewer gate, and usage analytics to detect drift

Contract Coordination

RI and DCS must cut over to the new payload schema in a coordinated release

Mitigate: additive-only schema changes; feature-flag staged rollout

Concurrent Edits

Simultaneous edits to a single article require clear "edit in progress" UX

Mitigate: pessimistic locking acceptable for MVP; clear lock indicator in UI

Storage Growth

Snapshot retention for every version increases storage over time

Mitigate: define a retention policy; only full snapshots for approved versions
