EU — European Commission Publishes Updated MDCG Guidance on Clinical Evaluation Under MDR 2017/745
Published: Jun 18, 2026 Region: Europe Issuer: European Commission Level: Finalized
🏥 Medical Devices Finalized 🇪🇺 European Union MDR 2017/745

The European Commission has published an updated MDCG guidance document (MDCG 2026-3) providing revised recommendations for clinical evaluation under Regulation (EU) 2017/745 on Medical Devices (MDR). This guidance supersedes the previous MDCG 2020-6 and reflects lessons learned from the first years of MDR implementation.

Key Changes

  • Clarified requirements for demonstrating clinical benefit versus state-of-the-art comparators
  • New section on post-market clinical follow-up (PMCF) study design expectations
  • Updated equivalence pathway requirements, narrowing acceptable device comparison scope
  • Additional guidance on sufficient clinical evidence for Class III and implantable devices
  • Annex with worked examples for common medical device categories

Manufacturers placing medical devices on the EU market will need to review their clinical evaluation reports (CERs) against the updated criteria. Notified Bodies have indicated they will begin applying the updated guidance during conformity assessment from Q4 2026.

For more information about this topic, please visit Emergo by UL Solutions.

Impact Assessment

The revised guidance is expected to affect approximately 4,200 manufacturers currently holding EU Certificates of Conformity for Class IIb, Class III, and implantable devices. Key areas requiring attention include:

  • CER updates to reflect new equivalence criteria (deadline: within next PSUR cycle)
  • PMCF plan revisions to align with updated study design expectations
  • Review of benefit-risk analyses against updated state-of-the-art benchmarks
  • Documentation of any literature search methodology changes

Manufacturers are advised to initiate gap analyses promptly to identify areas of their existing clinical evaluation documentation requiring revision before Notified Body review cycles beginning in late 2026.
